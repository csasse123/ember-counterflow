What research actually says
Standard spotting models (Tarifa, Albini, Koo, WRF-Fire) are downwind. Fire-induced reverse air under a wind-tilted plume is real CFD (Meroney, He et al.) — lee inflow feeds the fire. There is no separate operational “reverse firebrand” product model.

This lab emulates: 3D Boussinesq NS + Tarifa Lagrangian drag + Langevin subgrid turbulence \(\mathbf{u}'\). Bulk goes with free-stream; a few dense brands that sample reverse + turbulence can go retro. Details: docs/PHYSICS.md.
