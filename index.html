Ember Counterflow

Ambient wind alone cannot send embers upwind. A real fire solves the Navier–Stokes equations with buoyancy: the plume drives a ground in-draft that can reverse local flow. Embers follow that field — not a painted wind arrow.

Equations (2D, browser): stream-function / vorticity Navier–Stokes + Boussinesq buoyancy + heat equation with a fire source. Same structure as simplified plume CFD (see FDS low-Mach NS / Boussinesq plume literature).

How reverse flow appears: heat → rising plume → mass continuity → ground in-draft. If in-draft > Uw on the lee side, u < 0 (cyan). Embers integrate drag + gravity on that field and can land upwind.

Tip: preset Demo reverse (moderate wind, strong fire). If wind is too strong, reverse pockets vanish — as in reality.
