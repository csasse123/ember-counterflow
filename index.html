What actually happens in a fire
Almost every firebrand rides with the free-stream wind (left → right) and spots downwind. Only one or two heavy pieces fall into the fire-induced reverse pocket (magenta) and fly backwards — that is reverse ignition on the green pads.

Air is solved with 3D Boussinesq Navier–Stokes; particles are Lagrangian on that field. Side cut and 3D share the same arrays.
